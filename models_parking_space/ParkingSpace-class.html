ParkingSpace

ParkingSpace class

An immutable snapshot of a single parking space.

Instances are generated by ParkingService.generateParkingSpaces and persisted via ParkingRepository. Availability figures (availableSpaces and previousAvailableSpaces) are refreshed periodically through ParkingService.refreshAvailability to simulate live occupancy updates.
